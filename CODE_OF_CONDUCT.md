# Code of Conduct

QueryForge contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, technically
grounded, and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and behavior, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.
- Respect security disclosure boundaries and data confidentiality.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner.
